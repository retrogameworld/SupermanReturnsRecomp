# Features

Everything here is optional and can be changed in-game from **PC SETTINGS**, or from the separate Settings app before you launch.

## Performance & display

- **Up to 4K** (720p / 1080p / 1440p / 4K, plus a 4K upscaled mode)
- **120 fps and uncapped** frame rates. The game's simulation still runs at its original 30 Hz, and rendering is smoothly interpolated between ticks, so the game speed and physics stay correct
- **Windowed, borderless or exclusive fullscreen**
- **FXAA** anti-aliasing, **texture filtering** up to 16x, and "Sharp" upscaling for lower resolutions
- **Draw distance** up to 3x the original: the city no longer pops in right in front of you at high flight speed
- **Low-end options:** a one-click **graphics preset** (Low / Medium / High / Ultra), a low-VRAM mode for cards under 6 GB, and a low-CPU mode
- Shaders and HD textures are prepared at start-up behind a small loading bar, so there's no stutter the first time you see something
- **FPS counter** option

## Visual upgrades

- **HD texture pack** (AI-enhanced, optional) and **HD videos** (AI-enhanced 4K intro and story movies, optional)
- **Cape fix:** the original cape flickered and tore. It now renders cleanly, and **cape smoothing** keeps it fluid at 120 fps
- **Fly into space:** the sky turns into space as you climb, with a cloud deck and Earth below
- **No more invisible wall at the edge of the world:** the hard fog wall around Metropolis is replaced by natural atmospheric haze. When you cross the mountains, a short white-out carries you out to open sea, and the **distant mountain silhouettes** stay visible on the horizon so you always know where the city is. Flying back in takes you to the edge of the mountains, so you never see the hollow back side of the map
- **Clean title screen** option

## Gameplay & quality of life

- **Save anywhere:** saving also remembers Superman's position
- **Cutscene skip:** hold START / OPTIONS for a second
- **Event cooldown:** set how often random city events interrupt free roam
- **Free-roam boundary:** original, extended, or off
- **Camera distance** options
- **Lock-on fix:** L2 lock-on stays available (the meteor level used to switch it off) and re-locks reliably
- **Target switch** on D-pad down: cycles left, right, up and down, so it finds targets above and below you too (great for the meteors)
- **Movie soundtrack** option: plays the film's score as the game's music
- **Vibration strength** setting
- Instant **Alt+F4** quit, an **EXIT GAME** button, and **PC SETTINGS** reachable straight from the pause menu
- Menu fixes: BACK works everywhere in the save-game and Metropolis/Fortress menus, with proper select and back sounds

## Controller

- **PlayStation button prompts** throughout the game (DualShock / DualSense icons), or the original Xbox prompts
- Works with any controller; prompts switch with a setting

## Cheats (all optional, off by default)

- **Fast flight:** hold R1 and click L3 to step from 1x up to **50x** speed (hold L3 to drop back to 1x). The game automatically eases you back to normal speed right before you hit the ground
- **Damage multiplier:** 2x / 3x / 5x / 10x / **one hit** (only affects enemies, not the city)
- **Fast forward:** speed up the whole game 2x-4x on a button
- The game's own cheats from a menu: **infinite stamina, infinite city health, all power-ups, unlock all extras**

## Settings

Everything above can be changed **in-game** from a full **PC SETTINGS** page (controller-friendly), or from a separate **Settings app** before launching.

## Fixes

- Fixed the stray old sound bursts that played when you pressed buttons
- Fixed flicker, menu frame drops and various audio glitches from the original running on modern hardware
- Lots of smaller stability fixes
